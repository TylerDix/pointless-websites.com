Why Tyler says it matters

Apple Intelligence

Apple Intelligence is built right into the phone — no extra app, no subscription. It's available on iPhone 16 and iPhone 16e and makes everyday things genuinely easier. Here's what it actually does:

  • Writing Tools In Mail, Messages, or Notes, the phone can proofread your text, rewrite it more clearly, or shorten a long message — right inside the keyboard, no copy-pasting.
  • Photo Cleanup Tap once to remove an unwanted person or object from a photo. The phone fills in the background automatically. Also creates personalized memory slideshows from your library.
  • Smarter Siri Siri now understands natural questions, knows what's on your screen, and can take actions in your apps — like finding a specific message or looking someone up in your contacts.
  • Priority Mail Mail automatically puts the messages that actually need your attention at the top, so you don't have to dig through your inbox to find what matters.
  • Notification Summaries Instead of a pile of alerts from one app, you get a plain-English summary. Less clutter, quicker to know what's going on.
  • Image Playground Describe any image in plain words and the phone draws it — useful for personalized cards, fun pictures, or something creative to send the grandkids.

Built in, free, and keeps improving with every iOS update.

The practical part

How to Get It

Four steps. Tyler recommends checking #1 first — it can save you a lot.

  1. 01

    Check carrier trade-in deals first

    T-Mobile and Verizon regularly offer the iPhone 16 for free (or close to it) when you trade in your current phone and sign up for an unlimited plan. These deals rotate — check T-Mobile and Verizon before doing anything else.

  2. 02

    Or buy direct from Apple

    Pick your phone, carrier, color, and storage all in one place. Pay over 24 months at 0% interest with Apple Card Monthly Installments. Visit apple.com/shop/buy-iphone.

  3. 03

    Want to save the most? Try Apple Certified Refurbished

    Like-new iPhones from Apple's own store at a discount — same one-year warranty as new. The iPhone 15 is a good pick here. apple.com/shop/refurbished/iphone.

  4. 04

    Apple Intelligence only works on iPhone 16 or 16e

    The AI features require iPhone 16 (any model), iPhone 16e, or iPhone 15 Pro. The regular iPhone 15 doesn't have them. Tyler recommends it — it's useful now and keeps getting better. More at Apple.com.
