The practical part

How to Get It

You're switching from a Motorola (Android to iPhone). Apple has made this easy — there's a free app that does the heavy lifting. The Apple Store can handle the whole thing in person, which is the simplest route.

  1. 01

    Best option: go to an Apple Store

    Tell them you're switching from a Motorola and they'll handle the transfer right there — contacts, photos, texts, email, all of it. You walk out with a phone that already feels like yours. Bring your Motorola charged. Find your nearest store at locate.apple.com.

  2. 02

    Or order online — Tyler will help with the transfer

    Buy new at apple.com, used at Swappa, or refurbished at Apple Certified Refurbished. Always buy unlocked. When it arrives, install Move to iOS on your Motorola — Apple's free app that transfers your contacts, photos, and messages over Wi-Fi. Tyler will walk you through it.

  3. 03

    Get a dark case (refurb buyers)

    If you went the refurbished route, grab a dark case from Amazon or Best Buy. Covers whatever color you got. Any basic silicone case works fine.

  4. 04

    Sign up for Mint Mobile

    Go to mintmobile.com/plans and pick a plan. The iPhone 17e uses eSIM — no physical SIM card, Mint will email you a QR code to activate. Tyler can help with this too.

  5. 05

    You're done

    Millions of people switch from Android to iPhone every year. Your contacts, photos, and accounts come right over. A few apps may need re-downloading, but Tyler will sort anything out.
