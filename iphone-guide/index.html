On the iPhone 16e

Apple Intelligence

Apple Intelligence is built into the phone — no extra app, no subscription. It's why Tyler recommends the 16e over a cheaper refurbished model. Here's what it actually does in daily use:

  • Writing Tools In Mail, Messages, or Notes, the phone can proofread your text, rewrite it more clearly, or shorten a long message — right inside the keyboard, no copy-pasting.
  • Photo Cleanup Tap once to remove an unwanted person or object from a photo. The phone fills in the background automatically. Also creates personalized memory slideshows from your library.
  • Smarter Siri Siri understands natural questions, knows what's on your screen, and can take actions in your apps — like finding a specific text message or looking someone up in your contacts.
  • Priority Mail Mail automatically puts the messages that actually need your attention at the top, so you don't have to dig through your inbox to find what matters.
  • Notification Summaries Instead of a pile of alerts from one app, you get a plain-English summary. Less clutter, quicker to know what's going on.
  • Image Playground Describe any image in words and the phone draws it. Good for personalized cards, fun pictures to send the grandkids, or just something creative.

How to Get It

This is easier than it sounds. The Apple Store can do most of the work for you right there — and if not, Tyler's got it covered.

  1. 01

    Best option: go to an Apple Store

    If the phone you want is available in store, this is the easiest path. Walk in, tell them what you want, and they'll set it up right there — including transferring everything from your old phone: your contacts, photos, apps, text messages, all of it. You walk out with a phone that already feels like yours. Find a store at apple.com/retail.

  2. 02

    Or order online from Apple

    If they don't have it in store, order at apple.com/shop/buy-iphone/iphone-16e or the refurbished store. Select "unlocked" so it works with Mint Mobile. When it arrives, Tyler will help get everything transferred — it takes about 20 minutes and you don't have to do anything except keep your old phone nearby.

  3. 03

    Get a dark case (refurb buyers)

    If you went the refurbished route, grab a dark case from Amazon or Best Buy. It'll cover whatever color you got. Any basic silicone case works fine — doesn't need to be fancy.

    Sign up for Mint Mobile

    Go to mintmobile.com, pick a plan, and get a SIM card sent to you. The 10 GB or unlimited plans run about $15–$30/month when you pay three months at a time. No contract — cancel anytime. Tyler can walk you through this step too if needed.
