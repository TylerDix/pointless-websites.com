The practical part

How to Get It

You're moving from a Motorola, which means switching from Android to iPhone. Apple has made this easy — there's a free app that does the heavy lifting. The Apple Store can handle the whole thing in person, which is the simplest route.

  1. 01

    Best option: go to an Apple Store

    If the phone you want is available in store, this is the easiest path by far. Tell them you're switching from a Motorola and they'll handle the transfer right there — contacts, photos, text messages, email accounts, all of it. You walk out with a phone that already feels like yours. Bring your Motorola and make sure it's charged. Find your nearest store at locate.apple.com.

  2. 02

    Or order online — Tyler will help with the transfer

    Order new at apple.com/shop/buy-iphone/iphone-17e, used/unlocked at Swappa (from ~$464), or refurbished at the Apple Certified Refurbished store. Buy unlocked. When it arrives, install Apple's free Move to iOS app on your Motorola from the Play Store — it moves your contacts, photos, videos, email, and messages over Wi-Fi. Tyler will walk you through it. Keep both phones nearby and charged.

  3. 03

    Get a dark case (refurb buyers)

    If you went the refurbished route, grab a dark case from Amazon or Best Buy. It'll cover whatever color you got. Any basic silicone case works fine — doesn't need to be fancy.

  4. 04

    Sign up for Mint Mobile

    Go to mintmobile.com/plans and pick a plan. Current pricing: 6 GB/$15 · 17 GB/$20 · 23 GB/$25 · Unlimited/$30 — per month, paid three months at a time. The iPhone 17e uses eSIM (no physical SIM card) — Mint Mobile supports this fully, and will email you a QR code to activate service right on the phone. No contract, cancel anytime. Tyler can walk you through this step too.

  5. 05

    You're done — and it's more straightforward than it sounds

    Millions of people switch from Android to iPhone every year. Apple built Move to iOS specifically so this isn't painful. Your contacts, photos, and accounts come right over. A few apps may need to be re-downloaded from the App Store, but Tyler will take care of anything that needs sorting out.
