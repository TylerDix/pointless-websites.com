Tyler Says: This Is the Big Deal

What Is Apple Intelligence?

Apple Intelligence is Apple's built-in AI — available on iPhone 16 and iPhone 16e. It's not a separate app or subscription. It's baked right into the phone and makes everyday things genuinely easier. Here's what it can do for you:

✍️

Help Writing Anything

Your phone can proofread your emails, rewrite a message more clearly, or shorten a long text — all right inside Mail, Messages, and Notes. Just tap and it handles it.

📸

Magic Photo Cleanup

Tap once to erase an unwanted person or object from a photo. It fills in the background automatically. It also creates beautiful memory slideshows from your photo library.

🗣️

A Much Smarter Siri

The new Siri understands natural questions, knows what's on your screen, and can take actions in apps for you — like sending a message or looking something up in your contacts.

📬

Smart Email Inbox

Mail automatically spots the messages that need your attention and puts them at the top, so you never miss something important buried under junk.

🔔

Cleaner Notifications

Instead of a pile of alerts from the same app, you get one short summary. Less clutter, more clarity — at a glance you know what's going on.

🎨

Create Custom Images

Describe any image in plain words and your phone draws it. Great for personalized cards, fun illustrations, or just something creative to send the grandkids.

Apple Intelligence is built in and free — no extra subscription. It keeps getting better with every iOS update. Only available on iPhone 16 models and iPhone 16e.

Getting It Done

How to Get the Best Deal

A few simple steps can save you a lot of money. Here's the order Tyler recommends.

  1. 1

    Check carrier trade-in deals first — they can be huge

    T-Mobile and Verizon regularly offer the iPhone 16 for free (or nearly free) when you trade in an old phone and switch to an unlimited plan. These deals change monthly, so check both. Visit T-Mobile iPhone deals or Verizon iPhone deals to see what's running today.

  2. 2

    Or buy direct from Apple — easy and straightforward

    Apple's website lets you pick your carrier, color, and storage all in one place. You can use Apple Card Monthly Installments to pay over 24 months with 0% interest. Apple.com/shop/buy-iphone →

  3. 3

    Want to save the most? Go Apple Certified Refurbished

    Apple's own refurbished store sells like-new iPhones at a discount — with the same warranty as a new phone. The iPhone 15 is a great pick here if budget is the main concern. Apple Certified Refurbished iPhones →

  4. 4

    If you want Apple Intelligence, get the iPhone 16 or 16e

    Apple Intelligence only works on iPhone 16, iPhone 16 Plus, iPhone 16 Pro, iPhone 16 Pro Max, iPhone 16e, and iPhone 15 Pro models. The regular iPhone 15 does not have it.
